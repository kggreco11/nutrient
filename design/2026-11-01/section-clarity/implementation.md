# Home grouping and navigation clarity

The open home layout made progress, shortfalls, and the diary difficult to distinguish and left navigation too implicit for a new user.

## Changes

- Group today’s rings and macro bars in one neutral surface with a clear progress heading and date.
- Group remaining nutrients separately, with the count, contributor drilldowns, food suggestions, and an explicit link to the complete nutrient list.
- Keep today’s logged foods in their own surface. Put “View & edit food log” before the entries so it remains discoverable with a long diary.
- Add a chevron to the tappable nutrient coverage label; keep calories a static readout. Macro and shortfall rows retain contributor navigation and chevrons.
- Replace the unlabeled hamburger with “Menu.” Add labeled links to nutrient trends and saved foods/meals farther down home without introducing tabs.
- Preserve the neutral palette, native typography, original mark, scan/quick-log priority, Dynamic Type layout, and isolated chart selection state.

Native model/regression checks and Debug builds for Simulator and the paired physical iPhone passed. No fixture data is written to the real diary.
