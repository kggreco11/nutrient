# Mobile award references

Researched 2026-11-01. These are relevant awarded examples, not an objective ranking of the world's best mobile interfaces. Award status and project-description claims are separated from our proposed application to Nutrient. Apple examples and the proposed visual direction are in the [Apple brief](apple-awards-and-direction.md).

## Oura app — Instrument

**Verified award:** Indigo 2026 Digital Design of the Year; Gold in UX, Interface & Navigation and Interactive Design; Silver in Apps. The organizer credits Instrument and Oura. [Indigo entry](https://indigoaward.com/winners/11522).

**Designer account:** Instrument describes clearer information hierarchy, meaningful color, refined typography and three levels of health visualization: daily summary, focused metrics, and deeper exploration. [Instrument case study](https://www.instrument.com/work/oura-app).

**Our interpretation:** Keep today's coverage and logging immediate, make a nutrient tap reveal its contributors, and reserve detailed history for exploration. Color should communicate nutrient state consistently rather than simply decorate every surface.

## MyJCB — JCB / FOURDIGIT

**Verified award:** iF DESIGN AWARD 2025 winner, User Experience division. JCB calls the category Communication UX; the current iF catalog labels the project Retail/Banking UX. Do not call it an iF Gold winner. [JCB's April 24, 2025 announcement](https://www.global.jcb/en/press/2025/202504241700_products.html), [iF project record](https://ifdesign.com/en/winner-ranking/project/myjcb-app/696672).

**Source account:** iF describes a personalized credit-card app shaped by user feedback and a configurable balance of security and convenience. JCB describes interviews, surveys, improved visibility/accessibility, transaction sorting, and search. Its renewal launched in 2023; 2025 is the award year.

**Our interpretation:** Use one highly legible primary total, restrained supporting data, and direct routes into the entries behind a number. Personal style should coexist with predictable controls and readable figures. This is a reference for dense personal data, not a visual template for a nutrition app.

## Pice — Carla Pan Design Studio

**Verified status:** Listed in Red Dot's Brands & Communication Design 2025 winners catalog as a Health Solution. The page carries the Red Dot award mark and links to the designer's winner profile. It does not identify the project as Best of the Best; do not claim that tier. [Red Dot project record](https://www.red-dot.org/project/pice-85129).

**Organizer account:** Red Dot describes a health app using daily records and a clear interface designed to be approachable to older users. This is the organizer's project description, not evidence validating the app's medical claims or real-world performance.

**Our interpretation:** A health dashboard benefits from clear state, a modest number of decisions per screen, and large, readable controls. We should evaluate the actual mobile experience rather than copy polished presentation boards.

## How to use the awards

- Indigo's Oura entry is the closest subject match: making complex health data understandable across daily and longer views.
- iF provides a wider product/UX lens; its MyJCB entry illustrates information-dense personal data supported by research.
- Red Dot's mobile/interface catalog is useful for comparative visual references, with care to distinguish shipped products from concepts and award tiers.
- Awards provide references, not proof that a particular treatment will work for our users. Prioritize legibility, accessible text sizes, truthful data states, native interaction quality, and measured touch performance when adapting a pattern.
