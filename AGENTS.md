# Nutrient

For every UI change or new feature, read [.interface-design/system.md](.interface-design/system.md) before implementation. Apply its layout, typography, nutrition semantics and interaction checks to changed screens; verify them in the native iPhone build before reporting completion.

The product UI is native SwiftUI. Browser studies under `design/` are references and prototypes. Preserve the diary and historical recipe snapshots when changing presentation or meal editing.
